Transparent, Local Dentist in Georgetown

Meet Dr. Abdallah Ifraitekh:
Bringing 24 Years of Dental Excellence to Georgetown

Dr. Abdallah Ifraitekh brings decades of dental experience to serve Georgetown families. A graduate of Boston University Henry M. Goldman School of Dental Medicine, Dr. Ifraitekh has pursued extensive training in various specialties including oral surgery, endodontics, prosthodontics, periodontics, dental implants, and laser dentistry.

This comprehensive expertise allows him to provide a wide range of treatments under one roof, ensuring continuity of care for his patients. Dr. Ifraitekh's journey in dentistry has been driven by his passion for helping patients achieve optimal oral health through personalized, transparent care.

A Team That Treats You Like Family in a Spa-Like Environment

At 1460 Dental Studio, our team is committed to creating a warm, welcoming atmosphere where patients feel like family, never just a number. We work together to ensure your dental experience is comfortable and stress-free from scheduling to treatment completion.

Our office features a natural, earthy design with spa-like amenities including a beverage station, headphones, TVs, and warm towels that transform traditional dental visits into a relaxing experience. We've carefully designed our practice to reduce anxiety and make dental care something you can actually look forward to.

Transparent, Comprehensive Care Tailored to Your Needs

1460 Dental Studio specializes in providing transparent dental care with a focus on cosmetic dentistry, emergency services, and restorative treatments. We believe in taking a conservative approach, preserving natural tooth structure whenever possible while achieving excellent outcomes.

What sets us apart is our commitment to complete transparency in treatment and pricing—we take the time to thoroughly explain all options and associated costs, eliminating the surprise bills that many people fear when seeking dental care.

Whether you're looking for professional whitening, emergency pain relief, or a complete smile makeover, Dr. Ifraitekh's expertise ensures exceptional results with your comfort and budget in mind.

Experience the Difference at Georgetown's Newest Dental Home

Ready to experience a different kind of dental care? We invite you to join the 1460 Dental Studio family and discover how comfortable, transparent dentistry can be. Our Georgetown practice is now welcoming new patients with our special introductory offer of $89 for an exam, cleaning, and x-rays.

We also provide free second opinions if you're unsure about treatment recommended elsewhere. To schedule your appointment with Dr. Ifraitekh and our caring team, call us today at (000) 000-0000. We look forward to helping you achieve your healthiest, most beautiful smile.
